Doorstep Pickup & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: November 2, 2026

1. Service Time Slots

Pickups and deliveries operate 7 days a week across three primary slots: Morning (07:00 AM - 11:00 AM), Afternoon (01:00 PM - 04:00 PM), and Evening (05:00 PM - 09:00 PM).

2. Free Delivery Minimum

Doorstep pickup and delivery are 100% free on all orders with a total bill value of ₹200 or higher. For orders below ₹200, a standard nominal rider convenience charge of ₹40 is applied.

3. Standard vs. Express Turnaround

Standard turnaround is 24 to 48 hours depending on fabric requirements (dry clean items require 48 hours for solvent evaporation). Express 24-Hour delivery is available for urgent requirements with an add-on fee of ₹149.
